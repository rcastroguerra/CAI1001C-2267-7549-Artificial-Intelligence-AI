# Lesson 1: Demystifying AI and the Evolution of AI

## Introduction

The concept of intelligent machines has long captured the human imagination. From being a mere element of science fiction to becoming a tangible reality, AI is transforming the world around you.

In this lesson, you will explore AI’s rich history. You will uncover its origins, learn about the pioneers who laid its foundations, and dispel common misconceptions about what AI can do.
